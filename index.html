The Broke to B.L.I.N.G. Declarations

Crystal Archie

The Broke to B.L.I.N.G. Declarations is a faith-filled collection of Scripture-inspired declarations for the seasons when life feels broken, uncertain, or hard to rebuild.

Use these words to pause, pray, and speak truth over the areas of life that need hope and renewal, from faith and identity to family, health, finances, and purpose. Each declaration is a reminder that a difficult season does not define your whole story, and with God, you can believe again and take your next step.

Open the guide on your phone, choose a section that speaks to where you are today, and declare it over your life. Hear Crystal read every declaration and prayer in her own voice.
